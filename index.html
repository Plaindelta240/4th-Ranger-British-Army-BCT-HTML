Six Section Battle Drills — PREWAR
P — Preparation: Correct kit, right radio frequency confirmed before the operation.
R — Reaction to Effective Fire: "I'm up, I'm seen, 2 steps left or right, I'm down."
E — Enemy Location: Report format — e.g. "Enemy spotted West, bearing 200°, in front of Red Roof Building."
W — Winning the Fire Fight (commanded by 2IC):
› Deliberate Rate: 10 rds/min — 1 aimed shot every 6 seconds.
› Rapid Rate: Up to 30 rds/min — 1 aimed shot every 2 seconds, to gain fire superiority.
› Watch & Shoot: Deliberate rate; individual fires when enemy is positively identified.
A — Attack: Bounding and Peeling used to close distance. Charlie Fire Team is normally the attacking element; Delta provides covering fire.
R — Re-Org: All-round defence, cover arcs, do NOT drop guard. 2IC collects CASAM report from all callsigns — give accurate ammo state and health status.
